Privacy statement

Northwind Digital (Warre Peeters, enterprise number BE1045.273.285, Drongensesteenweg 99, 9000 Ghent, Belgium) respects your privacy. Here is what data we use, why, and what your rights are.

What data

Publicly available business information about sole traders and companies: business name, activity, address or town, phone number and, where public, a general email address. Sources: open map data (OpenStreetMap), public business registers and open government data. If you contact us, also what you tell us. Calls with our AI assistant are recorded and transcribed; this is said at the start of every call.

Why

To show you, as a business, a sample website and contact you about it (legitimate interest: business-to-business prospecting), and to answer your questions and follow up appointments.

How long

As long as needed for that purpose. If you ask us not to contact you again, we keep only your phone number or email address on an internal block list, so you really receive nothing more.

Who with

We never sell your data. We use services for email (Google Workspace), hosting of the sample sites and telephony; they process data only on our behalf.

Your rights

You can object at any time, ask for access, or ask us to erase your data and your sample page: email info@northwind-digital.com, call +32 488 22 29 43 or reply STOP to our email. You can also complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be).

Cookies

This website uses no cookies and no trackers.
