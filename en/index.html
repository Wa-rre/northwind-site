How it works

How it works

A website doesn't have to be a months-long project. We do the work; you just check it's right.

  1. 1

    We build a sample

    Using what's publicly known about your business (name, services, area, phone), we build a sample website, just for you.

  2. 2

    You take a look

    You get a link to your sample. Look at it on your phone or computer, whenever suits you. Want something changed? Just tell us.

  3. 3

    We put it live

    If you want to go ahead, we put the site live on your own domain (e.g. yourbusiness.com). If you like, we look after it afterwards too.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I pay for the sample?

No. The sample is free and you're not committed to anything.

I already have a Facebook page. Do I need a website?

A Facebook page is a good start, but people searching Google for a tradesperson nearby mostly see websites. A website works alongside your Facebook, not instead of it.

Do I need to write texts or send photos?

No. We prepare everything. You can always add your own photos or texts.

How long does it take?

The sample already exists when you get it. After your yes, the site is usually live within a week.

How did you get my details?

We use publicly available business information, such as your business name, services, area and phone number, from open sources and registers. More in our privacy statement.

I want my sample page removed.

No problem: email or call us and we remove the page and your details straight away. You can also reply STOP to our email.
